# Cyristal N. Joseph — Portfolio

This portfolio showcases my work across cybersecurity, investigations, privacy, regulatory compliance, and research through a modern, interactive user experience. It features technical projects, publications, professional experience, and curated writing that reflect my interest in the intersection of cybersecurity, law, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies. Each section is structured to communicate real-world capability and continuous  growth. 

---

## **✨ Key Features**

- **Experience & Resume:** Presents organizations I’ve worked with alongside a downloadable resume.
- **Skills by Specialty:** Organizes capabilities across cybersecurity investigations, privacy and legal analysis, and technical systems.
- **Filterable Credentials:** Lets visitors browse certifications by category and open supporting certificates or badge pages.
- **Selected Project Showcase:** Combines GIF previews, technology lists, and flip-card summaries with repository and live-project links in a scrolling carousel.
- **Independent Writing:** Highlights Narrative Damage and links directly to my published articles on Substack.
- **Contact & Opportunities:** Shares my availability details and direct links to LinkedIn, GitHub, and email.
- **Responsive Layout:** Adjusts navigation, project cards, and section layouts for phones, tablets, and desktop screens.

---


## **🛠️ Tech Stack**

- **HTML5** – Organizes the portfolio into clear sections for my background, experience, skills, certifications, projects, publications, and contact details, using headings, images, and links that help visitors find their way around.
- **CSS3** – Creates the portfolio’s colors, typography, spacing, animated effects, and card designs while adjusting the layout so the content remains readable on phones, tablets, and desktop screens.
- **JavaScript** – Makes the portfolio respond to visitors through the animated introduction, rotating text, flip cards, project carousel controls, and certification filters that help people explore my work.
- **Flexbox & CSS Grid** – Arrange cards, buttons, and section content into rows and columns that can resize or stack as the available screen space changes.
- **Custom Assets (GIFs, Icons)** – Give projects animated previews and add recognizable images and symbols to help visitors identify organizations, skills, and contact options throughout the portfolio.
